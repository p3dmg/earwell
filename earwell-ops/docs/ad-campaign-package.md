## 9. CREATIVE BRIEF SUMMARY

---

### Brand Voice
Passionate. Expert. Accessible. Love, Marriage and Basketball speaks to fans who feel underserved by mainstream sports media. The tone is never condescending — it assumes the audience knows basketball and respects that. Think "smart friend who watches every game" not "corporate sports broadcaster."

### Visual Identity
- **Primary colors:** Deep purple, bright gold, white
- **Typography:** Bold, athletic sans-serif for headlines; clean readable body
- **Photography style:** Authentic game photography, behind-the-scenes creator content, clean data/dashboard visuals
- **Avoid:** Stock photo basketball imagery that looks generic, over-produced corporate aesthetics

### Core Message Hierarchy
1. **Primary:** Women's college basketball is exploding — and we cover all of it, automatically.
2. **Secondary:** Consistency is the moat. Daily content. Every conference. No burnout.
3. **Tertiary:** Join the community that actually follows this sport at the level it deserves.

### Emotional Triggers
- **Fans:** Pride in their team finally getting coverage. Belonging to a community that gets it.
- **Creators:** Relief at escaping the manual treadmill. Excitement about sustainable growth.
- **Sponsors:** Confidence in a niche, growing, verifiable audience. FOMO on first-mover opportunity.
